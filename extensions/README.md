## 🚀 Two Ways to Use

### Option A: Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey Userscript (Fastest - 30 Seconds)
1. Install [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) (or Violentmonkey) in your browser.
2. Click **Create a new script** in Tampermonkey.
3. Paste the contents of [`extensions/autofill.user.js`](file:///c:/Project/ai-job-search/extensions/autofill.user.js).
4. Save (`Ctrl + S`).
5. Open any job link on **Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, Y Combinator, micro1, or Unstop**.
6. A floating `[⚡ 1-Click Fill]` button appears in the bottom right corner. Click it to fill 100% of standard fields in 0.05 seconds!
7. Click `[📝 Snippets]` to open the drawer with pre-written, audited engineering answers for TraceX, design systems, and frame budgeting.

---

### Option B: Chrome Extension (Unpacked)
1. In Chrome, Brave, or Edge, navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked**.
4. Select the folder: `c:\Project\ai-job-search\extensions\job-search-autofill`.
5. Pin the extension to your toolbar.
6. When on any job page, click the toolbar icon -> **⚡ 1-Click Autofill Form**.
